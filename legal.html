Cookie Policy

Cookies are small files a website asks your browser to store. Some sites use them heavily. This one does not.

1. What we set

Nothing. This site sets no cookies of its own. There is no analytics tag, no advertising pixel, no tracking script and no visitor profiling. That is also why you are not being shown a consent banner.

2. What the embedded services set

Some pages load content from other companies, and those companies may set their own cookies or use similar storage in your browser once that content loads:

  • Spotify — the player behind the album artwork. Spotify may store playback state and, if you are signed in to Spotify, recognise your account so you hear the full track rather than a preview. Governed by Spotify's own privacy and cookie policies.
  • YouTube — the video on the Watch section. YouTube may set cookies relating to playback and preferences. Governed by Google's policies.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr — these serve the typefaces and animation library the site uses. They receive your IP address as part of the ordinary request for those files. They do not set tracking cookies for us.

We have no control over and no access to whatever those companies store. If you want to know exactly what they do, their own policies are the authority.

3. How to control cookies

Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies, and lets you block third-party cookies specifically. Blocking them will not break this site — at most, an embedded player may refuse to load or may fall back to a 30-second preview instead of the full track.

4. Changes

If we ever add analytics or any other cookie-setting tool, we will update this page and add a consent banner before it starts running.
